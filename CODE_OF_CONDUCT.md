# Contributor Code of Conduct

LayerRail is an open infrastructure project. We want the project to be useful
to operators, developers, researchers, and teams who need cloud systems they can
understand, audit, and improve.

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces, including GitHub issues,
pull requests, discussions, review threads, documentation, community channels,
and project events.

## Our Standards

We expect contributors and maintainers to:

- Treat other people with respect, patience, and good faith.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on evidence, tradeoffs, and project
  goals.
- Welcome contributors with different experience levels, backgrounds, and
  operational contexts.
- Give and receive review feedback constructively.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.
- Protect sensitive information, including credentials, customer data,
  vulnerability details, and private infrastructure metadata.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private communications or personal information without consent.
- Sharing secrets, exploit details, or sensitive infrastructure data in public
  channels.
- Deliberately disrupting project work, review, or community discussion.
- Repeatedly pushing unrelated, off-topic, or hostile discussion after
  maintainers have asked for it to stop.

## Security-Sensitive Conduct

LayerRail manages cloud infrastructure. Contributors must treat operational and
security details with care.

Do not post credentials, API tokens, private keys, customer data, deployment
secrets, exploit chains, or unpatched vulnerability details in public issues or
pull requests. Use the process in the [security policy](.github/SECURITY.md)
for security reports.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take action to protect the project and its contributors. Actions
may include editing or removing content, closing discussions, declining
contributions, limiting participation, or blocking accounts from project spaces.

Enforcement decisions should be based on the behavior, its impact, and the need
to keep the project safe and productive.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe conduct that violates this policy, contact the
maintainers at support@layerrail.com. Include relevant links, screenshots, and a
short description of what happened.

Maintainers will review reports as privately as practical and will avoid
retaliation against anyone who reports concerns in good faith.
