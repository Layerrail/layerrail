## Recommended order

1. **Antler Nigeria** — best immediate accelerator fit for a Nigeria-based founder, provided the founder can attend the eight-week, full-time Lagos Sandbox beginning September 14, 2026.
2. **Microtraction** — best immediate African pre-seed form for a technical founder with an MVP; $100,000 stated investment.
3. **Emergent Ventures Africa** — best immediate non-dilutive route that accepts an individual and does not require incorporation or a pitch deck.
4. **Oui Capital / Ingressive Capital** — strong early-stage African funds once private traction, cap-table, round, and valuation data are confirmed.
5. **Y Combinator Fall 2026** — late applications remain accepted; no incorporation is needed to apply, but an accepted company must use a US, Canadian, Cayman, or Singapore parent.
6. **NLnet Restack/Fediversity successor calls** — strongest open-source alignment; NLnet reopens several calls on September 3, 2026. The exact funds in that reopening must be rechecked on that date.
7. **develoPPP Ventures** — revisit November 15 only after LayerRail has an eligible local entity, first revenue, an annual financial statement, and at least €100,000 in matching finance.
8. **Startup credits** — manage the existing Microsoft benefits, track the Google review, and resume AWS only after secure Builder ID recovery; use credits to reduce burn, not as fundraising traction.

## Upcoming non-dilutive opportunities

### NLnet / Open Internet Stack

- The general proposal page states that **new calls for several funds open September 3, 2026** and close **November 3, 2026 at 12:00 CEST**.
- Restack's own page still says the fund is being set up and **Coming soon**. The September 3 window is therefore a planning date, not yet a confirmed Restack-specific call.
- Restack is being established with €7 million for open internet-stack R&D. First proposals may request **€5,000–€50,000**; follow-on proposals can grow, with programme limits described in its guide.
- Restack explicitly includes decoupled infrastructure layers, service management, and deployment innovation. LayerRail’s AGPL control plane is strongly aligned.
- Applicants worldwide may apply, but a **clear European dimension is a knock-out criterion**, and EU/Horizon-associated applicants receive priority when proposals are equal.
- Milestones are paid after completion, not up front.
- NLnet’s formal GenAI policy permits assisted preparation only with disclosure and a full provenance log containing model, prompt dates/times, prompts, and unedited output. Its programme FAQ asks applicants to write applications themselves. The final application should therefore be founder-authored from a factual brief, or disclose every AI interaction completely.

Sources: [NLnet proposal schedule](https://nlnet.nl/propose/), [Restack](https://nlnet.nl/restack/), [eligibility](https://nlnet.nl/restack/eligibility/), [guide](https://nlnet.nl/restack/guideforapplicants/), [GenAI policy](https://nlnet.nl/foundation/policies/generativeAI/).

### develoPPP Ventures

- Current call is closed; the official page states the next call begins **November 15, 2026**.
- Provides **€100,000 non-dilutive matching capital** and possible top-up support.
- Nigeria is eligible, but a startup must be registered in the target country or commit to registration before funding starts; funding must be invested locally.
- Requires proof of concept, first revenue, at least one annual financial statement, no more than €2 million raised, break-even potential within three years, and matching finance of at least €100,000.
- LayerRail should not apply until these conditions are true and documented.

Source: [develoPPP Ventures](https://www.developpp.de/en/funding-programme/startups/).

## Legal-structure conclusion

No primary-source corporate registry record or definitive LayerRail incorporation document was found. The website footer says “LayerRail, Inc.”, but the public terms and privacy pages call themselves working summaries and provide no jurisdiction, registration number, registered address, or incorporation date. Investor forms should therefore use **“LayerRail (pre-incorporation)”** unless the founder produces a certificate from another jurisdiction.

This does not block Antler, YC, Emergent Ventures, or many founder-first investors. It does block the Nigeria Startup Label portal, which requires an RC number at account creation, and weakens applications to credit programmes that expressly require incorporation. The recommended financing path is to apply as a founder/project now and form an investor-compatible parent (commonly a Delaware C-corporation, subject to legal/tax advice) before accepting equity financing.
